How to ask for a quotation.

The more complete the request, the faster the quotation.

  1. Sizes and the tonnage of each, or the full bar bending schedule. You can prepare it in the weight calculator or RebarPlan.
  2. The grade: Grade 60 or Grade 80.
  3. Whether you need cutting to length, or epoxy-coated bars.
  4. Project city and delivery site, and payment method: direct payment or letter of credit.

Common questions

Which sizes are available?

Reinforcing bars in 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 25 and 32 mm, in 12 m lengths, in Grade 60 and Grade 80.

Can the bars be cut to a bending schedule?

Yes. Cutting to the lengths you need is available. Send the bar bending schedule or cut lengths with the quotation request.

Is epoxy-coated rebar available?

Yes, epoxy coating is available. Mention it in the quotation request with the sizes and quantities.

When is delivery?

Delivery is immediate after payment or after the letter of credit is opened.

Are test certificates available?

Yes, all test certificates are available.

Where does supply come from?

Al Rajhi Steel has two mills, in Riyadh and Jeddah, and branches in Riyadh, Jeddah, Qassim and Taif.
