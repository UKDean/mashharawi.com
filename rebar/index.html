Grade 60 or Grade 80.

Saudi Building Code SBC 304 is based on ACI 318, and Saudi drawings and contracts usually specify reinforcing bars to ASTM A615. Grade 60 and Grade 80 are named after their minimum yield strength in ksi; the metric names, Grade 420 and Grade 550, give it in MPa.

Minimum mechanical properties, ASTM A615 Grade 60 and Grade 80
PropertyGrade 60 (420)Grade 80 (550)
Minimum yield strength420 MPa550 MPa
Minimum tensile strength620 MPa725 MPa

The grade is the designer's decision: anchorage, lap lengths and the number of bars are calculated for the grade on the drawings, so changing it needs the designer's written approval. If the drawings call for weldable or seismic bars to ASTM A706, say so in the request. Figures from ASTM A615/A615M; always check the edition your contract names.

Before the material submittal.

Three things to confirm before you submit rebar for approval on a Saudi project.

  1. The standard and grade written in the contract and on the drawings, for example ASTM A615 Grade 60.
  2. The mill test certificate: heat number, grade, size, mechanical properties and chemical composition. All test certificates are available for our bars.
  3. Whether the project requires third-party testing, and how many samples.

What is the difference between Grade 60 and Grade 80?

Minimum yield strength: 420 MPa for Grade 60 and 550 MPa for Grade 80, under ASTM A615. Grade 80 is stronger, but the grade on the drawings is the designer's decision, and changing it needs the designer's written approval.

Which rebar standard do Saudi projects use?

Usually ASTM A615, or ASTM A706 for weldable and seismic bars, as written in the project contract and drawings. Saudi Building Code SBC 304 is based on ACI 318.

Do you supply 22 mm or 28 mm bars?

The listed sizes are 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 25 and 32 mm. If your drawings show 22 or 28 mm, mention it in the quotation request so it can be confirmed first.
