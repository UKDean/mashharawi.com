What the tool does

The generator turns your requirements into a structured request for quotation (RFQ) for Al Rajhi Steel reinforcing steel: customer, project, product lines, delivery and payment preference, with a summary and a printable document. It does not calculate or show prices.

When to use it

  • When you know the diameters, grades and tonnes you need and want a quotation.
  • To send the same clear request to the sales team by WhatsApp or email, or to attach it as a PDF.
  • To check a quantity list before asking for prices.

Step by step

1. Customer

Company name, contact person and phone number are required. Email, country and city are optional. A phone number needs 7 to 15 digits; spaces, + - and brackets are allowed. An email, if you give one, must be a valid address.

2. Project

Project name and location are required. Add the delivery site address and the required delivery date if you know them; a date in the past is refused. Choose a delivery schedule: single delivery, multiple deliveries, or to be discussed. Use the notes for anything the sales team should know, such as access or working hours.

3. Product lines

Press Add product line for each product. For every line choose the product type, then the diameter and grade, and enter the quantity in metric tons (a dot for decimals, up to 3 decimals). Nothing is pre-selected: the tool never assumes a diameter or grade.

  • There is no 22 mm, no coil and no plain round bar.
  • The 8 mm cold-rolled product is a separate category: choosing it sets 8 mm and Grade 40, and it is never mixed with Grade 60 or Grade 80.
  • If you change the product type, a diameter or grade that does not apply is cleared and you are told; nothing is replaced silently.
  • Each line can have its own delivery date and a short note. Remove a line with Remove line.
  • Two lines with the same product, diameter, grade and delivery date are flagged as a possible duplicate. They are not merged: combine them yourself, or add a note that explains the difference.

4. Commercial

Choose the payment terms you prefer: cash in advance, bank LC, or to be confirmed with sales. Final payment terms and product availability are confirmed by the Al Rajhi Steel sales team. Quantities below 30 t can be requested; full truckload requirements and extra transport costs may apply, and the summary reminds you.

5. Review

Press Preview RFQ. If anything is missing or invalid, a list of the problems appears; select one to go to the field. Only a complete, valid form produces the preview, so invalid data never reaches the RFQ. The preview shows the reference, the date, customer and project details, the product table with the total, delivery, payment preference, notes, the commercial notes and the disclaimer.

6. Print, copy or send

  • Print / Save as PDF prints only the RFQ document; choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog to keep a file.
  • Copy RFQ text copies the plain-text version shown under the buttons.
  • Open in WhatsApp and Open in email work only after you tick “I have reviewed this RFQ”. They open WhatsApp or your email application addressed to the Al Rajhi Steel sales contact and pass the RFQ text to it; the message reaches the sales team when you press Send there.
  • WhatsApp limit: a WhatsApp link can carry up to 4,000 characters; the count is shown under the RFQ text. A longer RFQ (with short line notes, typically from about 20–30 product lines in English or Arabic, or about 40 in Chinese) is never opened as a cut message: the complete text is copied instead, and you paste it into WhatsApp yourself or send the PDF from Print / Save as PDF.
  • Email limit: long RFQs, and almost all Arabic or Chinese RFQs, are too long to fill in through an email link. The complete text is copied first; the email then opens with the subject and a line asking you to paste it. Click in the message body and paste (Ctrl+V, ⌘V, or press and hold › Paste) before sending. If copying is blocked, the email does not open: the text is selected on the page for you to copy.
  • If you change the form after the preview, sharing waits until you press Update preview.

What the RFQ is, and is not

  • It is a request for quotation that you prepare. It is not a price offer, purchase order, sales contract or confirmation of availability.
  • The reference (for example RFQ-20261120-K7M2Q) is made in your browser to identify your request. It is not an Al Rajhi Steel quotation number.
  • Prices, final specifications, payment terms and delivery schedules are confirmed by Al Rajhi Steel.

Privacy: what stays in the browser and when it leaves

  • While you work: everything you type, the summary and the preview stay in this browser tab. There is no server, no account and no upload to this website.
  • Nothing is saved: closing or refreshing the tab clears the form.
  • When you press Open in WhatsApp: the RFQ text is put into a wa.me link, which opens WhatsApp’s website or app with the text filled in. From that moment the text has left this page and is with WhatsApp, even before you press Send.
  • When you press Open in email: the RFQ text (or, for a long RFQ, the subject and a paste note, with the full text on your clipboard) is passed to your email application. From then on it is handled by that application and your email provider.
  • When you copy: the text is placed on your device’s clipboard, where other apps on the device can read it.

Common mistakes

  • Entering kilograms instead of metric tons: 2500 means 2,500 t, not 2.5 t.
  • Using a comma for decimals: 12,5 is refused; type 12.5.
  • Choosing epoxy-coated bars without asking for availability: the RFQ marks them “availability to be confirmed”.
  • Splitting one product into many identical lines: combine them or explain the difference in a note.

Limitations

  • No prices, discounts or delivery dates are calculated or promised.
  • At most 50 product lines per RFQ.
  • Bending and cutting requirements are not part of the form; describe them in the notes, and they are confirmed before quotation.
  • The RFQ is not saved: print it, save it as PDF or copy it before closing the tab.
