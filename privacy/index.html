Policy · last updated 16 November 2026

Privacy policy

This is a personal website. It has no accounts and no comments. Its one form is the rebar quotation request. This page lists what is still collected when you visit, and by whom.

Who runs this site

mashharawi.com is the personal website of Omar Mashharawi, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. For anything about this policy or your data, email info@mashharawi.com.

What the site itself collects

Nothing that identifies you, unless you send a quotation request. There are no accounts and no tracking cookies set by the site itself.

  • Calculators. The rebar and steel section calculators run entirely in your browser. The diameters, lengths and quantities you enter are not sent anywhere; the section calculator only downloads its own table of section dimensions from this site.
  • Contract check. The contract tool reads the file you choose inside your browser to extract its text. The file is not uploaded to this site. It uses two open-source libraries: pdf.js, served from this site, and mammoth, loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com with an integrity check. If you then paste the text into ChatGPT or another AI service, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Quotation request form. If you send the form at /quote/, you send your name, company, position, mobile, email (optional), project, delivery city, tonnes per diameter, grade, dates and notes. This is covered in the next section.
  • Which page or post brought you. If you arrive from a link that carries utm_ tags (for example from a LinkedIn post), the tags and the first page you opened are kept in your browser (localStorage) and sent only with a quotation request. When you click a WhatsApp link, one event with the page and those tags is counted; it contains no name or number.
  • Email. If you email an address at mashharawi.com, the message is received through Cloudflare Email Routing and delivered to a Gmail inbox; replies may be sent through Brevo. Your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

Quotation requests

Omar Mashharawi works in reinforcing steel sales at Al Rajhi Steel. The quotation form on this site lets you ask him for an official Al Rajhi Steel quotation.

  • What is sent. The fields you fill in, the page you sent the form from, and, if you arrived from a tagged link, the utm_ tags and the first page you opened on this site. Nothing else about you or your device.
  • Where it is stored. In a Google Sheet, through a Google Apps Script, in a Google account controlled by Omar for this purpose. Google processes it under Google's privacy policy. If the storage moves to a system approved by Al Rajhi Steel, this page will say so.
  • Who is told. Omar receives an alert with your request through Telegram (a bot that only he receives), and, if he turns it on, by email. Telegram processes the message under Telegram's privacy policy.
  • What it is used for. Only to contact you about your request and to arrange the official quotation, which Al Rajhi Steel issues through its own commercial process. Your details are passed to Al Rajhi Steel's sales process for that purpose. They are not sold, and no marketing messages are sent to you automatically.
  • How long it is kept. While your request and any order that follows are being handled, and then only as long as Al Rajhi Steel's commercial records require. You can ask for deletion at any time.
  • Your rights. You can ask what is held about you, correct it, or have it deleted, by writing to info@mashharawi.com or omar.mashharawi@rajhisteel.com.
  • If sending fails. The page shows a summary that you can send yourself by WhatsApp or email; nothing is kept in the site.

Services that process data when you visit

Cloudflare (hosting, security, analytics)

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, including automated checks that tell browsers apart from bots. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies and without building a profile of you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The site shows no advertising.

Google Fonts

The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Apps

The iOS and Android apps listed on this site have their own privacy pages: Steel Section Calculator, Tubes and Arabic Crossword.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.
  • Email info@mashharawi.com to ask what data from your emails or quotation requests is held, or to have it deleted.
  • Clear this site's stored data in your browser to remove the saved utm_ tags.

Changes

If the site starts using a new service that handles visitor data, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
