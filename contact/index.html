Rebar quotations

For reinforcing steel from Al Rajhi Steel, message me on WhatsApp Business or email my work address, omar.mashharawi@rajhisteel.com. A quotation is fastest when the message includes:

  • the bar sizes and the quantity of each, in tonnes or pieces, or the bar bending schedule;
  • the grade (Grade 60 or Grade 80) and whether you need cutting, bending or epoxy coating;
  • the delivery city and the date you need the steel on site.
