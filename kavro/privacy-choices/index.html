Who may receive submitted URLs

If you request a check, the KAVRO reputation service forwards the address to the databases enabled on it: Google Web Risk or Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal and URLhaus. They may keep it under their own policies. You can turn checks off entirely in Settings.

Delete your information

  • History: open Settings and choose Clear History.
  • Everything on your device: delete the app.
  • Emails to us: write to omar@mashharawi.com and ask us to delete them.
  • Our service keeps nothing that identifies you, so no account or request needs deleting there. For an address already sent to a provider, contact that provider.

Contact the owner

Omar Mashharawi, omar@mashharawi.com

Report a privacy concern

Email omar@mashharawi.com with what you saw and which version you use. Please don't include private links or passwords. If you think you've found a security flaw, tell us privately at the same address. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
