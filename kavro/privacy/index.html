Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

KAVRO reads QR codes on your device. This version doesn't collect your data and makes no network requests of its own.

Who is responsible

KAVRO is created and owned by Omar Mashharawi, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. This policy covers the KAVRO apps for iPhone and Windows and the KAVRO pages on mashharawi.com. On the Apple App Store, the seller shown on the listing is the holder of the Apple Developer account that publishes KAVRO. Omar Mashharawi remains its creator and owner, and is responsible for this policy.

The short version

Everything KAVRO does happens on your device: reading QR codes, the security analysis, scan history and settings. KAVRO doesn't collect, upload, sell or share your data, and it has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Image processing

QR images are processed on your device, with Apple's Vision framework on iOS and the ZXing.Net library on Windows. Images are never uploaded and never saved by KAVRO.

Camera

On iPhone, KAVRO can scan QR codes live with the camera, using Apple's VisionKit, only after you tap Scan with Camera and iOS has your permission. It stops when a code is found or you close the scanner. The video is analyzed on your iPhone, is never saved or uploaded, and no photos are taken. The Windows version uses your webcam to read QR codes, only after you start it from the scan screen and Windows has your permission. It stops when you leave that screen or a code is found. Video is processed in memory on your device, one frame at a time. It is never saved or uploaded. If you refuse permission, you can still read QR codes from image files.

Photo library

On iOS, KAVRO uses the system photo picker, so it doesn't ask for access to your library. It only receives the image you select.

Scan history

History is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, history is stored locally on your device and isn't sent to us. It contains the decoded text and the scan date, and never the image.

KAVRO removes Wi-Fi passwords and payment identifiers before saving a new entry. Other decoded text is saved as it appears, and can still be sensitive, such as a link containing a private token. Entries saved by an earlier version stay on your device unchanged until you clear them, and sensitive values in them are masked on screen. You can turn history off or clear it in Settings. A device backup may include it.

Network requests

This version of KAVRO makes no network requests of its own and works fully offline. It never opens anything by itself. Opening a link, handing off to a payment app or reporting a link happens in another app, only after you confirm, and that app's own policy applies.

Planned online checks

A later version may offer optional online reputation checks of a link you choose. Before any such version is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what would be sent, to whom, and how long it would be kept, and a check would run only when you ask for it.

Data deletion

Clear history in Settings, or delete the app to remove all local data. We hold no data about your use of the app, so there's nothing for us to look up or delete. To delete emails you sent us, write to omar@mashharawi.com.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use these rights. We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Children

KAVRO isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect their personal data.
