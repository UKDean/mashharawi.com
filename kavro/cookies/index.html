Cookies

Last updated: 5 November 2026

KAVRO's own pages set no cookies, and the apps don't use cookies.

KAVRO pages

These pages contain no scripts, no advertising and no analytics code of their own. Fonts and styles are served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact third parties to show them.

Cloudflare

mashharawi.com is served by Cloudflare. To protect the site, Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some cases, for example to tell browsers apart from bots. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits to the site without cookies. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The apps

The KAVRO iOS app doesn't use cookies. Reputation checks run in a private session that keeps no cookies. Settings are stored on your device.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. These pages work without them.

The rest of mashharawi.com

Other pages on this website are covered by the site's own privacy page at https://mashharawi.com/privacy/
