Security Disclaimer

Last updated: 5 November 2026

KAVRO helps you make a better decision before you open something. It doesn't make that decision safe.

What you need to know

  • No security scanner can guarantee detection of every malicious URL.
  • A clean result doesn't guarantee that a website is safe. It only means no known threat was found.
  • Unknown or unchecked URLs must not be considered safe.
  • KAVRO doesn't replace antivirus software or other device protection.
  • KAVRO can't guarantee the prevention of financial fraud.
  • KAVRO never approves financial transactions. It only shows what a code says and can hand off to another app after you confirm.
  • You must independently verify payment recipients. A code can name anyone.

Why results can be wrong

Databases only know what has been reported, so new or targeted attacks may be missing. Sites can change after a check, or show different content to different visitors. Local checks are heuristics and produce both false alarms and misses. A domain-only check can miss a threat on one page.

What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Confirmed MaliciousA threat database returned a confirmed match.
SuspiciousLocal checks or a weak database signal found warning signs.
No Known Threat DetectedAt least two independent databases answered and neither lists it. This is not proof of safety.
Not CheckedThere isn't enough information. Treat it as unknown.
