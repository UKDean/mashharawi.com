KAVRO

Know Before You Open.

KAVRO reads QR codes with your camera or from your photos, shows what each one would do, and never opens anything without asking you first.

Available now on Windows. Coming soon to iPhone. Get it from the Microsoft Store

See it working

What it inspects

  • Look-alike links

    Brand impersonation, homograph domains, disguised IP addresses and shortened links.

  • Payment codes

    Scheme, recipient and amount as written in the code, with a clear warning that the recipient isn't verified.

  • Application links

    Store pages, custom schemes, banking and wallet links, APK downloads and configuration profiles.

  • Wi-Fi settings

    Open networks, outdated security and tempting network names.

  • Risky downloads

    Links that lead straight to installers and configuration files.

Clear, honest results

  • Suspicious

    The on-device checks found warning signs. Review them before you continue.

  • Not Checked

    No warning signs were found, but nothing checked the link online, so treat it as unknown. Online reputation checks are planned for a later version.

Private by design

  • Images are read on your device and are never uploaded or saved.
  • Nothing opens automatically, and payments are never started or approved.
  • Works offline. This version sends nothing over the network on its own.
  • No accounts, advertising or tracking.

KAVRO isn't an antivirus or a device malware scanner, and it can't promise complete protection. Read the Security Disclaimer
