Document Approval Assistant

Project approval, vendor registration, prequalification and technical submittal packages, from the company's certificate library to one ordered PDF.

Sign in

Open the tool

For Al Rajhi Steel and Saudi Benaa staff only, with a company email.

Account

Request an account from the sign-in page. A code is sent to your company mailbox, then an administrator approves your access.

Language

Arabic first with right-to-left layout; English one click away.

What it does

From the certificate library to a package ready for approval.

I

Document library

PDF, Word, Excel and images, including scanned and combined files. Arabic and English OCR; one combined PDF is split into its certificates.

II

Rule-based validity

Valid, expiring soon, expired or missing a date. Expired documents are left out by default, and a missing date never counts as valid.

III

From request to package

Type the request and attach the customer's checklist. Documents are matched strictly and merged into one ordered PDF with a cover and index.

IV

Form filling

PDF, Word and Excel forms filled from controlled company data. Signature and stamp fields are never filled.

V

Review and approval

Draft, review, approval and submission, with an audit log of every step and an internal compliance report.

VI

SharePoint sync

Copies new files from the company's shared folder on a schedule, without ever deleting from the library.

Security and privacy.

Sign-in is limited to company email addresses, proven with a code sent to that mailbox. Files are kept on a private server with no public links, and no document is sent to any outside AI service. Every upload, edit, approval and export is logged.
