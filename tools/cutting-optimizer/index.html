How the optimizer works.

Every length is converted to whole millimetres, so there is no rounding drift. Pieces are only combined with pieces of the same diameter and the same grade.

Each cut removes the kerf. A bar cut into n pieces needs n cuts, or n−1 when the last piece ends exactly at the bar end, and the optimizer counts it that way.

It solves the linear relaxation of the cutting stock problem by column generation (Gilmore–Gomory), rounds that to whole bars, also runs three fast heuristics (sequential best-fill patterns, first fit decreasing and best fit decreasing), and keeps the plan with the fewest bars.

It then compares the plan with a lower bound: the larger of the Martello–Toth bound and the rounded-up linear optimum. When the two are equal, the result is marked proven minimum: no plan can use fewer bars, and with one stock length that is also the least waste. Otherwise it is marked as a heuristic result and shows the largest possible gap. It never claims an optimum it has not proved.

Waste is everything bought but not delivered as a required piece: kerf, any allowance, and offcuts. Offcuts at or above the length you set are listed as reusable; shorter ones are scrap.

Common questions

How do I reduce rebar cutting waste?

Combine pieces of the same diameter and grade so each 12 m bar is filled as completely as possible. The optimizer searches those combinations for the whole job at once and reports the waste percentage, so you can compare it with what the site is losing today.

Is the result really the minimum?

When the page says proven minimum, yes: the number of bars equals a mathematical lower bound, so no plan can use fewer. When it says heuristic, the plan is good but not proven, and the page shows how many bars it could be above the minimum at most.

Can I paste my bar bending schedule from Excel?

Yes. Copy the columns diameter, length in metres, pieces and optionally grade, and paste them. You can correct any line before you calculate. The file never leaves your computer.

What are reusable offcuts?

Leftovers long enough to use on another element. You set the minimum length, 1 m by default; the plan lists every reusable offcut by length and count, and anything shorter is counted as scrap.

Is anything I enter sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is stored. The WhatsApp button asks first, then copies a summary and opens the chat; you decide whether to send it.

Does this give me a price?

No. It gives the bars and tonnes to order. For a quotation, send them to me and I will reply with an official Al Rajhi Steel quotation.
