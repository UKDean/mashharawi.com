What it does

From the drawing to the bending machine.

I

Bar bending schedule

Members, shapes, lengths and quantities in one BBS.

II

Cutting plan

Cuts arranged for the least waste from stock bars.

III

Shop sheet & labels

Printable sheets for the shop floor and labels for bundles.

IV

Rebar weights

Weight by diameter and for the whole schedule.

V

Export

Excel, PDF, or straight to email.

VI

Three languages

Arabic, English and Simplified Chinese.

Privacy and support.

RebarPlan runs entirely on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere: no account, no tracking, no ads. Your projects are saved where you choose.

Questions, suggestions and bug reports: info@mashharawi.com, or an issue on GitHub. Results are for planning and estimating: check cut lengths with the engineer of record before cutting steel. MIT licence. Other tools: all tools.

Free, open source, and built from fifteen years of rebar orders.
