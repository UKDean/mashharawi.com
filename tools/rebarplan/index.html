RebarPlan.

A bar bending schedule and a cutting plan in one program. Enter the bar marks, or import them from Excel, and RebarPlan works out the cut lengths, the weight per diameter, and how to cut the job from 12 m stock bars with the least waste. Free, offline and open source.

Download for Windows or Mac View the source

Version 1.0. It installs as a normal desktop application with its own icon. The installers are not code-signed yet: on Windows, SmartScreen may show a notice the first time (choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”); on a Mac, right-click RebarPlan in Applications and choose “Open” the first time.

What it does.

  • ScheduleCut lengths by shapeBS 8666:2005 shape codes 00, 11, 21 and 51, or ACI 318 / Saudi SBC 304 with out-to-out dimensions. Legs below the BS 8666 minimum are flagged.
  • ScheduleWeight per diameterPieces, metres and kilograms for each size, at 7,850 kg/m³.
  • CuttingFewest stock barsCutting patterns per diameter from 12 m bars, or any mix of 6, 9, 12 and 18 m. Tested to stay within one bar of the theoretical minimum.
  • CostSaving in riyalsEnter a price per tonne to see the steel cost and what the plan saves against cutting each mark on its own.
  • SiteWorkshop sheet and labelsA printable cutting list for the operator, and a label for every bundle.
  • ExportExcel, PDF or emailExport the schedule and plan to Excel, save the report as PDF, or open a new email with the file attached.

Privacy and support.

RebarPlan runs entirely on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere: no account, no tracking, no ads. Your projects are saved where you choose.

Questions, suggestions and bug reports: info@mashharawi.com, or an issue on GitHub. Results are for planning and estimating: check cut lengths with the engineer of record before cutting steel. MIT licence. Other tools: all tools.
