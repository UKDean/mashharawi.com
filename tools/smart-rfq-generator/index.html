Tools / RFQ generator · In the browser · Free

Prepare a clear rebar request for quotation.

List the products, diameters, grades and tonnes you need, check the summary, and get a clean RFQ to print or send. It prepares the request only: no prices, and nothing is sent until you send it.

What this is. A form that turns your requirements into a structured request for quotation for Al Rajhi Steel reinforcing steel. It does not price anything, does not reserve stock and is not an order.

Privacy. While you fill in and preview the RFQ, everything stays in this browser tab: nothing is uploaded to this website or saved, and closing or refreshing the tab clears the form. When you press Open in WhatsApp or Open in email, the RFQ text is passed to that application, which handles it from then on.

      What the RFQ does and does not represent

      • It is a request for quotation prepared by you. It is not a price offer, purchase order, sales contract or confirmation of availability.
      • The reference number is made in your browser to identify your request. It is not an Al Rajhi Steel quotation number.
      • Products and options reflect Al Rajhi Steel’s range. Epoxy-coated availability, final specifications, payment terms and delivery are confirmed by the sales team.
      • Quantities below 30 t can be requested; full truckload requirements and extra transport costs may apply.
